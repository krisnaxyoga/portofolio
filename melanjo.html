Case Study · E-commerce

Melanjo

An e-commerce platform built for Indonesian MSMEs (Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises). Features integrated shipping cost calculation via RajaOngkir, complete admin dashboard, and mobile-responsive design.

Target MSME Businesses
Development Time 3 Months
Role Solo Developer
Type E-commerce Platform
PHP Laravel MySQL HTML CSS JavaScript Bootstrap jQuery RajaOngkir API

Screenshots

Challenges

Building a robust online store presents several key challenges — particularly providing accurate shipping cost calculations via RajaOngkir API and ensuring optimal performance and security within the Laravel framework. MSMEs often struggle with:

  • Limited technical knowledge to set up and manage e-commerce platforms
  • High costs associated with existing e-commerce solutions
  • Complex shipping cost calculations for Indonesian logistics
  • Lack of integrated inventory management systems
  • Difficulty in managing customer orders and payments
  • Need for mobile-responsive design for modern consumers

Solution

I developed this online store independently over three months — covering planning, development, testing, and deployment. Laravel 10 was chosen as the primary framework, with RajaOngkir API integrated for real-time shipping cost calculations.

Complete E-commerce Solution

Full-featured online store with product catalog, shopping cart, order management, and customer accounts.

RajaOngkir Integration

Real-time shipping cost calculation for all major Indonesian courier services including JNE, TIKI, and Pos Indonesia.

Secure Admin Panel

Comprehensive admin dashboard for managing products, orders, customers, and business analytics.

Mobile Responsive

Fully responsive design that works seamlessly across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Payment Integration

Multiple payment options including bank transfer, e-wallet, and cash on delivery.

Business Analytics

Built-in analytics and reporting tools to track sales, customer behavior, and business growth.

Impact & Results

Melanjo successfully empowered MSMEs to establish their online presence and compete in the digital marketplace:

  • Cost-Effective Solution: Significantly reduced the barrier to entry for MSMEs wanting to sell online
  • Streamlined Operations: Automated inventory and order processing save time and reduce errors
  • Accurate Shipping: RajaOngkir integration ensures precise shipping cost calculations
  • Enhanced Customer Experience: User-friendly interface and mobile responsiveness improve satisfaction
  • Business Growth: Enables MSMEs to reach wider markets and increase sales through online channels
  • Data-Driven Decisions: Analytics tools help business owners make informed decisions

Conclusion

Melanjo represents a comprehensive e-commerce solution specifically designed for the Indonesian MSME market. By focusing on ease of use, cost-effectiveness, and local market needs (such as RajaOngkir integration), the platform bridges the gap between traditional small businesses and the digital economy.

This project demonstrates my ability to understand market needs, implement complex integrations, and deliver practical solutions that drive real business value.
